# Session 9 — Random Loot

Roll a d10 or choose one to three finds for the Black Market, Vargheim, the frozen ship, or an old expedition cache. These are optional homebrew items; none is required to reach the Sterrenput.

## 1. Rimehook Axe

*A dwarf smith forged the hook of this axe to bite into frozen rigging as easily as bone.*

**Effect:** Once per short rest, when you hit a creature with this axe, you can reduce its speed by 10 feet until the end of its next turn.

**Damage:** 1d8 slashing.

## 2. Whitewake Harpoon

*A frayed red line still runs from the harpoon's shaft to the belt of a sailor who never came home.*

**Effect:** It has a 30-foot attached rope; once per turn after you hit a Medium or smaller creature, you can use a bonus action to pull it 5 feet toward you if it fails a DC 13 Strength saving throw.

**Damage:** 1d6 piercing when used with one hand, or 1d8 piercing with two hands.

## 3. Blackglass Knife

*Dark veins inside the blade shimmer like a star seen through deep water.*

**Effect:** Once per long rest, hold the knife against a page to make erased or invisible ink on that page glow for 1 minute; it does not reveal writing that was never there.

**Damage:** 1d4 piercing.

## 4. Mirror-Ice Shield

*The shield's polished face carries a hairline crack shaped like a frozen fjord.*

**Effect:** It functions as a shield, granting +2 AC while used; once per long rest, you can use your reaction to reduce cold damage you take by 1d6.

## 5. Stormglass Lantern

*Blue fire trembles inside this lantern even when the wind outside is perfectly still.*

**Effect:** It burns for 8 hours without oil; once per long rest, watching its flame for a minute grants advantage on one Survival check to navigate a storm or blizzard.

## 6. Dwarven Snowshoes

*Tiny iron runes have been stamped into the broad wooden frames of these well-used shoes.*

**Effect:** While wearing them, nonmagical deep snow does not slow your walking speed; they offer no protection against thin ice.

## 7. Frostline Rope

*This fifty-foot coil stays supple where ordinary rope would freeze stiff and snap.*

**Effect:** Once per short rest, you can use it to gain advantage on one Athletics check to climb an icy surface or pull another creature across one.

## 8. Sealhide Mittens

*The worn palms smell faintly of smoke from Vargheim's longhouse hearth.*

**Effect:** You can handle freezing metal without bare-skin contact; once per short rest, you have advantage on one Dexterity check to manipulate gear in extreme cold.

## 9. Second-Tide Compass

*Its needle shivers just before the ice groans under a changing tide.*

**Effect:** Within 30 feet of moving seawater beneath ice, the needle trembles; it does not show ice thickness or a safe route.

## 10. Captain's Signal Whistle

*A captain's initials are almost worn away from the silver mouthpiece.*

**Effect:** Its call can be heard up to 300 feet away even in strong wind, and creatures that hunt by sound can hear it as well.

## Suggested placement

| Location | Suitable finds |
|---|---|
| **Black Market** | Blackglass Knife, Second-Tide Compass |
| **Vargheim** | Rimehook Axe, Mirror-Ice Shield, Dwarven Snowshoes, Sealhide Mittens |
| **Frozen ship or expedition remains** | Whitewake Harpoon, Stormglass Lantern, Frostline Rope, Captain's Signal Whistle |

The Blackglass Knife and Second-Tide Compass provide clues, but they do not replace the map pieces or the dwarf guides. Nidhrym's hoard remains his property if the party wins safe passage through flyting.
