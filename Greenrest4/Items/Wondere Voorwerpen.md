# Wondrous Items — Greenrest4

---

### **1. The Echostone**
- **Type:** Wondrous Item | **Rarity:** Rare | **Attunement:** Yes *(Druid, Ranger, or telepathic creature)*
- **Description:** A fist-sized dark grey stone, faceted smooth. Faint purple shimmer. Always cold. Vibrates near mycelium.
- **Network Link:** Cast *Sending* through any connected mycelium network. Underground: unlimited range. Overground: 1 mile.
- **Ground Sense:** Passively sense creatures touching the ground within 30 ft. — location only, not thoughts.
- **Aberration Ward:** Resistance to psychic damage from aberrations.
- **Speak with the Network (1/day):** Cast *Speak with Plants* as a bonus action. Underground, includes living mycelium threads. Duration: 10 minutes.
- **Warning:** Vibrates and warms within 120 ft. of Vraaketh or a root node. Vraaketh can sense it when active in his domain.
- *Found via: Sidequest — The Myconid Colony (Choice C)*

---

### **2. Driftglobe — Shaft 7**
- **Type:** Wondrous Item | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Attunement:** No
- **Description:** A glass orb with a soft white inner glow. A crude pickaxe is scratched into the glass — the mark of Shaft 7. Left behind by Barreck.
- **Light:** As a bonus action, sheds bright light 20 ft., dim light 20 ft. beyond. No concentration.
- **Float (1/day):** As a bonus action, the orb hovers up to 5 ft. from you and follows automatically. Can also be set to hover in place.
- *Found in: Part 3B — Barreck's Resting Place*

---

### **3. Scholar's Loupe**
- **Type:** Wondrous Item | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Attunement:** No
- **Description:** A small copper magnifying glass with a quartz lens. Engraved: *"G.K. — Truth is smaller than you think."* Once owned by Grimkoog.
- **Aberration Sense:** When held over objects or creatures tied to aberrations, vibrates faintly in the handle.
- **Archivist:** Advantage on Intelligence (History) checks about Underdark geology, aberration lore, and forgotten Underdark civilizations.
- *Found via: Part 8 — Grimkoog (deal or combat)*

---

### **4. Sovereign's Blessing**
- **Type:** Amulet | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Attunement:** No
- **Description:** A dried spore wreath set in a ring of petrified wood. Faintly smells of rain and moss. Woven by the Sovereign of The Silent Ring.
- **Spore Blessing (1/day):** Advantage on one Nature or Survival check, or one saving throw against poison or disease.
- **Network Sense:** Near living mycelium, instinctively sense whether the area is healthy or corrupted. No DC — DM discretion.
- *Found via: Sidequest — The Myconid Colony (any ending)*
