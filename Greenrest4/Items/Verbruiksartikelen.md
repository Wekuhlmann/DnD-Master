# Consumables — Greenrest4

---

### **1. Stone Salt**
- **Type:** Consumable — Antitoxin | **Rarity:** Common | **Doses:** 3
- **Description:** Old dwarven mine disinfectant. White crystalline powder in a stone vial. Sharp mineral smell.
- **Effect:** Functions as *Antitoxin*, but specific to spores, fungi, and mycotoxins. Advantage on Constitution saves against spore attacks and mushroom poisons for 1 hour.
- *Found in: Part 3C — The Shaft (top of the crane, DC 13 Perception)*

---

### **2. Glowcap**
- **Type:** Consumable — Light Source | **Rarity:** Common | **Qty:** 1d4+1 per searcher
- **Description:** A bioluminescent mushroom bud the size of a cherry. Blue-green pulsing light. Not magical — just alive.
- **Effect:** Sheds dim light in a 5 ft. radius for 8 hours after picking. Can be clipped, hung, or pocketed safely.
- **Trade Value:** 3 sp each at Splintermarkt.
- *Found in: Part 5 — The Mushroom Grotto (DC 11 Nature)*

---

### **3. Deepblue Cap**
- **Type:** Consumable — Herb | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Doses:** 1
- **Description:** A rare mushroom with a deep blue cap. Grows only where underground river water has long soaked the earth. Tastes absolutely awful.
- **Effect:** Eating costs one bonus action. Grants Advantage on the next Constitution saving throw. Expires on next long rest if unused.
- *Found in: Part 5 — The Mushroom Grotto (DC 14 Nature — one only)*

---

### **4. Deepstone Liquor**
- **Type:** Consumable — Drink | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Doses:** 1
- **Description:** A dark brown bottle sealed with pressed fungal wax. Smells of deep earth and smoke. Dwarven brew from Splintermarkt. Not recommended for surface-dwellers.
- **Effect:** Grants Advantage on one Constitution saving throw of your choice. Expires on next long rest if unused.
- *Received from: Brek Knobbelsteen (Splintermarkt, payment for Myconid scouting)*

---

### **5. Grell Tentacle Fluid**
- **Type:** Consumable — Poison | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Doses:** 2d4
- **Description:** Clear, pale yellow fluid harvested from a slain Grell's tentacle base. Odourless. Stable for 24 hours outside the Grell body.
- **Effect:** Applied to a weapon (1 min). On a hit: DC 11 Constitution save or target is *Paralyzed* for 1 round.
- **Expires:** After 24 hours, loses potency.
- **Trade Value:** 15 gp/dose at a Splintermarkt alchemist.
- *Harvested from: Part 6 — Grell Carcasses (DC 13 Medicine, 10 minutes)*
