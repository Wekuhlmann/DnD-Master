# Trade Goods & Miscellaneous — Greenrest4

*No magical effect unless noted. Valuable as currency, crafting material, or story hooks.*

---

### **1. Luminesite Crystals**
- **Type:** Trade Good — Raw Material | **Rarity:** Uncommon | **Value:** ~5 gp each
- **Description:** Small crystals from the Gas Spore shaft walls. Bioluminescent in the dark — yellow, orange, or deep purple depending on composition. Used by Underdark craftsmen in magical lanterns and inlay work.
- **Use:** Raw crafting material. No direct magic. Skilled artisans can set them in light sources, jewellery, or simple focus items.
- **Sold at:** Splintermarkt — deep gnome crystal traders pay well.
- *Found in: Part 3C — The Shaft (loose in walls, DC 11 Investigation — 1d4+1 per searcher)*

---

### **2. Varrin's Stone Signet**
- **Type:** Jewellery — Ring | **Rarity:** Common | **Value:** 8 gp *(right buyer)*
- **Description:** A simple dark stone ring, rough-cut but solid. A pickaxe is engraved in the face — a miners' guild mark. Found wedged under the bridge, preserved by cold water.
- **Story Value:** "Varrin Dikzak" is chiselled into the bridge railing in Part 3A. A dwarf at Splintermarkt who knows the name pays up to 8 gp or trades information. Barreck may recognise it.
- *Found in: Part 3A — The Bridge (wedged in the masonry below, DC 12 Perception)*

---

### **3. Grell Beak**
- **Type:** Trade Good — Crafting Material | **Rarity:** Common | **Value:** Requires processing
- **Description:** A large chitin beak segment from a slain Grell. Hard, lightweight, and surprisingly sharp at the edges. Smells of damp and old meat — fades after a day of drying.
- **Crafting:** A Splintermarkt smith can fashion it into a functional dagger. Takes 2 days and 5–10 gp. The result is a plain dagger, but recognisable as Grell-material to those who know.
- *Harvested from: Part 6 — Grell Carcasses (always available after the fight)*

---

### **4. Dwarven Offering Vase**
- **Type:** Curiosity — Religious Object | **Rarity:** Common | **Value:** 20 gp *(collector or devout)*
- **Description:** A small dark-glazed vase bearing the seal of a dwarven mine goddess. Left behind in the overseer's chamber amid the mushrooms — a farewell offering. Contains 12 sp and a small gold disc.
- **Contents:**
  - 12 sp in old dwarven coin
  - Gold offering disc with a woman's face: 6 gp raw gold, up to 20 gp for a dwarven religious community or antique dealer
- *Found in: Part 8 — Overseer's Chamber (behind the largest mushroom, DC 15 Investigation)*
