# Quest Items — Greenrest4

*Quest items cannot be sold and hold no mechanical effect unless specified. Do not destroy or discard without DM approval.*

---

### **1. Echostone Fragment**
- **Type:** Quest Item — Crafting Component | **Rarity:** Rare
- **Description:** A palm-sized stone chip. Dark grey, faint purple shimmer. Always colder than the surrounding rock. Holding it causes a low buzz in your teeth.
- **Resonance:** Slowly attracts mycelium over time. After one week carried: a small mushroom appears at the bottom of the bag each morning.
- **Crafting:** Required to craft *The Echostone*. Needs a ritual by the Sovereign of The Silent Ring.
- **Detectable:** Grimkoog and Vraaketh can sense its resonance. Vraaketh feels it if used actively in his domain.
- *Found in: Part 8 — Overseer's Chamber (chest, after solving the puzzle)*

---

### **2. Sending Stone**
- **Type:** Quest Item — Magic Item | **Rarity:** Uncommon
- **Description:** One half of a matched pair of smooth communication stones. The other half is missing. Somewhere in the Underdark — or above — someone holds it.
- **Effect:** Speak into it; the holder of the other half hears your words regardless of distance. Max 25 words. 1/day.
- **Quest Hook:** The other half's location is unknown. Possible owners: Duergar, Freek, or someone who knew the Overseer.
- *Found in: Part 8 — Overseer's Chamber (chest)*

---

### **3. Overseer's Journal**
- **Type:** Quest Item — Prop | **Rarity:** Common
- **Description:** A small leather journal with a copper clasp. Most pages are blank or water-damaged. Four pages remain legible.
- **Key Entries:**
  - *"Day 3 after the find. The mushrooms are back. Growing toward my room."*
  - *"Day 12. It sings. That is the only word I have."*
  - *"Day 19. Barreck hears it too. He wants to go toward it. I forbade him."*
  - *"Day 31. Shaft 7 is sealed. I take the key with me."*
- *Found in: Part 8 — Overseer's Chamber (chest)*

---

### **4. Grimkoog's Notebook**
- **Type:** Quest Item — Prop | **Rarity:** Common
- **Description:** A crumpled notebook. Handwriting shifts between clear and frantic, as if the writer was losing focus. Full of geological sketches and observations.
- **Key Entry:** A rough sketch of the echostone vein, captioned: *"The frequency points east. Always east."*
- *Received from: Part 8 — Grimkoog (deal or found after combat)*
