# Bulette

*Large monstrosity, unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 17 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 94 (9d10 + 45)
**Speed** 40 ft., burrow 40 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 19 (+4) | 11 (+0) | 21 (+5) | 2 (-4) | 10 (+0) | 5 (-3) |

---

**Skills** Perception +6
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Tremorsense 60 ft., Passive Perception 16
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 5 (1.800 XP)

---

## Traits

***Standing Leap.*** The bulette's long jump is up to 30 feet and its high jump is up to 15 feet, with or without a running start.

---

## Actions

***Bite.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +7 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 30 (4d12 + 4) piercing damage.

***Deadly Leap.*** If the bulette jumps at least 15 feet as part of its movement, it can then use this action to land on its feet in a space that contains one or more other creatures. Each of those creatures must succeed on a **DC 16 Strength or Dexterity saving throw** (target's choice). On a failed save, the creature takes 14 (3d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage plus 14 (3d6 + 4) slashing damage and is **Knocked Prone**. On a successful save, the creature takes half the damage and isn't knocked prone.

---

## Notes

> Een bulette (ook wel "landhaai" genoemd) is een begraven roofdier dat via zijn tremorsense prooien opzoekt. Het opduiken vanuit de grond is zijn kenmerkende aanval.
>
> **Tactiek:** Burrow naar een positie onder de party, gebruik Deadly Leap om meerdere spelers te raken, bijten het zwaarst gewonde doelwit.
