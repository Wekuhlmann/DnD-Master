# Seress, Weaver of the Roots

*Medium humanoid (elf), neutral evil* — **Baas / Homebrew**

---

**Armor Class** 12 (15 met Mage Armor)
**Hit Points** 45 (6d8 + 12)
**Speed** 30 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 10 (+0) | 14 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 16 (+3) | 14 (+2) | 12 (+1) |

---

**Saving Throws** Int +5, Wis +4
**Skills** Arcana +5, Nature +5, Perception +4
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 14
**Languages** Common, Elvish, Sylvan, Druidic
**Challenge** 4 (1100 XP)

---

## Traits

***Groene Heiligdom (Lairfunctie).*** Binnen 5 ft. van het groene altaar heeft Seress voordeel op saving throws tegen magie.

---

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** Seress gebruikt één cantrip en één Root-Staf aanval.

***Root-Staf.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 5 (1d6 + 2) bludgeoning damage plus 3 (1d6) necrotic damage.

***Chill Touch (Cantrip).*** *Ranged Spell Attack:* +5 to hit, range 120 ft., one target. *Hit:* 9 (2d8) necrotic damage, and the target can't regain hit points until the start of Seress's next turn.

---

## Spells

Spellcasting ability: Intelligence (spell save DC 13, +5 to hit)

| Slots | Spells |
|-------|--------|
| 1st (3 slots) | *ray of sickness* (2d8 poison, DC 13 Con of poisoned), *entangle* |
| 2nd (2 slots) | *misty step* |
