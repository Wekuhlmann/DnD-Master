# Grell

*Medium aberration, neutral evil*

---

**Armor Class** 12
**Hit Points** 55 (10d8 + 10)
**Speed** 10 ft., fly 30 ft. (hover)

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 15 (+2) | 14 (+2) | 13 (+1) | 12 (+1) | 11 (+0) | 9 (-1) |

---

**Skills** Perception +4, Stealth +4
**Damage Immunities** Lightning
**Condition Immunities** Blinded, Prone
**Senses** Blindsight 60 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 14
**Languages** Grell
**Challenge** 3 (700 XP)

---

## Traits

***Blind Beyond Blindsight.*** The grell can't use its blindsight to perceive creatures or objects more than 60 feet away from it, and is otherwise completely blind.

---

## Actions

***Multiattack.*** The grell makes two attacks: one with its tentacles and one with its beak. It can use its beak only against a creature it is grappling.

***Tentacles.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 10 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (2d4 + 2) piercing damage. The target must succeed on a **DC 11 Constitution saving throw** or become **Poisoned** for 1 minute. While poisoned this way, the target is also **Paralyzed**. The target can repeat the saving throw at the end of each of its turns, ending the effect on itself on a success. The target is also **grappled** (escape DC 15). The grell can have only one creature grappled at a time.

***Beak.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +4 to hit, reach 5 ft., one grappled creature. *Hit:* 11 (2d8 + 2) piercing damage.

---

## Notes

Verscheen in: **Sessie 4 (Greenrest4)**

> **Tactische noot:** Een verlamd wezen wordt aangevallen met Advantage en alle aanvallen binnen 5 ft. zijn automatisch een Critical Hit. De Grell stijgt na het grijpen van een prooi naar 10 ft. om melee-aanvallen te bemoeilijken.
