# Grotere Gargoyle (Greater Gargoyle)

*Large elemental, chaotic evil* — **Homebrew Variant**

---

**Armor Class** 16 (natural armor)
**Hit Points** 75 (10d10 + 20)
**Speed** 30 ft., fly 60 ft.

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 18 (+4) | 11 (+0) | 15 (+2) | 6 (-2) | 11 (+0) | 7 (-2) |

---

**Damage Resistances** Bludgeoning, Piercing, and Slashing from nonmagical attacks that aren't adamantine
**Damage Immunities** Poison
**Condition Immunities** Exhaustion, Petrified, Poisoned
**Senses** Darkvision 60 ft., Passive Perception 10
**Languages** Terran
**Challenge** 4 (1100 XP)

---

## Traits

***Valse Verschijning.*** Beweegloze gargoyle is ononderscheidbaar van een standbeeld.

***Stenen Huid.*** Weerstand tegen alle schade die niet magisch of adamantijn is.

---

## Actions

***Multiaanval.*** De grotere gargoyle maakt drie aanvallen: één beet en twee klauwen.

***Beet.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 8 (1d8 + 4) piercing damage.

***Klauw.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) slashing damage.

***Steengooi (Recharge 5–6).*** *Ranged Attack:* +6 to hit, range 40/80 ft., one target. *Hit:* 13 (2d8 + 4) bludgeoning damage.
