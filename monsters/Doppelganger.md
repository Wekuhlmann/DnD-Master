## Traits

***Gedaanteverwisseling (Shapechanger).*** The doppelganger can use its action to polymorph into a Small or Medium humanoid it has seen, or back into its true form. Its statistics, other than its size, are the same in each form. Any equipment it is wearing or carrying isn't transformed. It reverts to its true form if it dies.

***Hinderlaag (Ambusher).*** In the first round of a combat, the doppelganger has advantage on attack rolls against any creature it surprised.

***Verrassingsaanval (Surprise Attack).*** If the doppelganger surprises a creature and hits it with an attack during the first round of combat, the target takes an extra 10 (3d6) damage from the attack.

---

## Actions

***Multiaanval.*** The doppelganger makes two Slam attacks.

***Slam.*** *Melee Weapon Attack:* +6 to hit, reach 5 ft., one target. *Hit:* 7 (1d6 + 4) bludgeoning damage.

***Gedachten Lezen (Read Thoughts).*** The doppelganger magically reads the surface thoughts of one creature within 60 feet of it. The effect can penetrate barriers, but 3 feet of wood or dirt, 2 feet of stone, 2 inches of metal, or a thin sheet of lead blocks it. While the target is in range, the doppelganger can continue reading its thoughts as long as the doppelganger's concentration isn't broken (as if concentrating on a spell). While reading the target's mind, the doppelganger has advantage on Wisdom (Insight) and Charisma (Deception, Intimidation, and Persuasion) checks against the target. A target may resist with a DC 13 Wisdom saving throw; on a success, the doppelganger cannot read that creature's thoughts again for 24 hours.

## Vleesamassa *(instabiele tussenfase)*

Wanneer een doppelganger tot 0 HP wordt gereduceerd, valt het lichaam niet neer — het zakt ineen tot een trillende, vormeloze massa vlees zonder gelaat of ledematen. Deze massa beweegt langzaam in de richting van andere gevallen doppelgangers.

| Eigenschap | Waarde |
|---|---|
| AC | 8 |
| HP | 10 |
| Snelheid | 10 ft. |
| Condition Immunities | Charmed, Frightened |

De massa heeft geen aanvallen en geen reacties. Als twee of meer massa's in hetzelfde vakje eindigen, smelten zij samen tot het **Blubbercollectief**.

*Spelers die een massa vernietigen vóór de samensmelting verminderen de maximale HP van het Blubbercollectief met 20 per vernietigde massa.*
