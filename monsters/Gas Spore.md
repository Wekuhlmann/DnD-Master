# Gas Spore

*Large plant, unaligned*

---

**Armor Class** 5
**Hit Points** 1
**Speed** 0 ft., fly 10 ft. (hover)

---

| STR | DEX | CON | INT | WIS | CHA |
|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|-----|
| 5 (-3) | 1 (-5) | 10 (+0) | 1 (-5) | 1 (-5) | 1 (-5) |

---

**Condition Immunities** Blinded, Deafened, Frightened, Prone
**Senses** Blindsight 30 ft. (blind beyond this radius), Passive Perception 6
**Languages** —
**Challenge** 1/2 (100 XP)

---

## Traits

***Death Burst.*** The gas spore explodes when it drops to 0 hit points. Each creature within 20 feet of it must succeed on a **DC 15 Constitution saving throw** or take 10 (3d6) poison damage and become **Poisoned** for 1 minute. While poisoned this way, a target that fails a **DC 10 Constitution saving throw** at the end of each of its turns gains one level of exhaustion. A creature that succeeds on the initial saving throw takes half damage and isn't poisoned.

***False Appearance.*** While motionless, the gas spore is indistinguishable from a beholder. *(DC 15 Investigation to tell the difference without observing movement.)*

---

## Actions

***Spores of Madness (Recharge 5-6).*** The gas spore ejects a puff of spores at one creature within 5 feet of it. The target must succeed on a **DC 10 Wisdom saving throw** or be **Frightened** of the gas spore until the end of the target's next turn.

---

## Notes

Verscheen in: **Sessie 4 (Greenrest4)**

> **DM-noot (Sessie 4 variant):** De Death Burst DC is verlaagd naar 12 en schade naar 2d6 voor een level 3 party. De sporen zijn niet agressief tenzij aangevallen of sterk verstoord.
